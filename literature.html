How the reading maps to the course

The textbooks cover the foundations and computation blocks. Chan et al. and Koop are the closest fit for the econometric framing; Gelman et al. (BDA) is the reference for general Bayesian modelling and diagnostics; Lambert is the gentlest entry point if the material is new to you.

The core papers anchor the applications block: Primiceri (2005) for time-varying VARs and state-space models, Giannone et al. (2021) for shrinkage in high-dimensional forecasting, Avramov and Zhou (2010) for Bayesian portfolio choice, and Hill (2011) for Bayesian causal inference.

The source PDFs are reading material; the site provides adapted explanations and derivations rather than reproducing the books.
